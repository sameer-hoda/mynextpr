THE ANALYSIS CRITERIA (View-Dependent - Apply Based on Step 0 Detection)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔴 FOR FRONT/BACK VIEW (Anterior/Posterior Angle):

1. HIP DROP / PELVIC OBLIQUITY (Trendelenburg Sign):
   • Visual Check: Is the waistband/shorts line tilted? Compare left vs. right hip height
   • RED Flag: If waistband is not horizontal → Weak gluteus medius on stance side
   • Geometric Test: Draw horizontal line across hips - any tilt = RED
   
2. KNEE VALGUS (Medial Collapse of Stance Leg):
   • Visual Check: Is the STANCE knee (weight-bearing leg) collapsing inward toward midline?
   • RED Flag: If knee center is medial to the ankle/foot line → High ACL/MCL injury risk
   • Geometric Test: Draw vertical line from hip to ankle - knee should be ON this line
   
3. CROSSOVER GAIT / STANCE WIDTH:
   • Visual Check: Are feet landing across the body's centerline (tightrope running)?
   • RED Flag: Feet crossing midline → IT band stress, hip instability
   • Geometric Test: Draw vertical centerline - feet should land on respective sides
   
4. ARM CARRIAGE (Upper Body Mechanics):
   • Visual Check: Are hands crossing the chest midline? Are shoulders elevated/tense?
   • RED Flags:
     - Hand crossing sternum → Rotational instability
     - High guard (hands at chest level) → Shoulder/trap tension
     - Asymmetrical swing → Compensating for lower body weakness
   • Geometric Test: Draw vertical centerline - hands should stay on respective sides
   
5. CARRIED OBJECTS & ASYMMETRY:
   • Visual Check: Is runner holding anything? Any left vs. right imbalance?
   • RED Flag: Phone, bottle, or gesture → Destroys natural arm swing mechanics

---

🔵 FOR SIDE VIEW (Lateral Angle):

1. FOOT STRIKE PATTERN:
   • Visual Check: At ground contact, is the toe pointed UP (dorsiflexed) or NEUTRAL?
   • RED Flag: Heel strike with dorsiflexion = HIGH braking force
   • GREEN Flag: Midfoot or forefoot strike with neutral ankle
   • Geometric Test: Look at shin angle - if >90° from ground = heel strike
   
2. OVERSTRIDE / TIBIAL ANGLE:
   • Visual Check: Is the foot landing AHEAD of the knee at initial contact?
   • RED Flag: Foot far in front creates braking force and knee stress
   • GREEN Flag: Foot landing under center of mass (hip)
   • Geometric Test: Draw vertical line from knee - foot should be near or behind this line
   
3. FORWARD LEAN (The Most Misunderstood Metric):
   • Visual Check: Draw imaginary line from EAR to ANKLE - what angle does it make?
   
   • RED FLAGS (Common Failures):
     ❌ VERTICAL (90°): No lean = No momentum = Braking with every step
     ❌ "SITTING IN THE BUCKET": Hips behind shoulders = Energy leak
     ❌ "HINGING AT WAIST": Bend from waist rather than ankles = Back strain
   
   • GREEN FLAG (Correct Form):
     ✅ SLIGHT FORWARD LEAN (5-10°): Entire body tilts forward like a falling tree from ankles
   
   • Critical Understanding: "UPRIGHT POSTURE" in running is a FLAW, not a virtue
     Most AI errors happen here - being vertical is BAD for running efficiency
   
4. KNEE DRIVE (Swing Leg Mechanics):
   • Visual Check: Is the swing leg heel pulling UP toward the glute (high recovery)?
   • RED Flag: "Shuffle" stride with low heel = Inefficient, slow turnover
   • GREEN Flag: Heel approaches glute = Optimal leg recovery
   • Phase Warning: Only assess this in SWING phase, not when foot is on ground
   
5. HEAD POSITION & NECK ALIGNMENT:
   • Visual Check: Is the chin parallel to ground? Is gaze focused forward?
   • RED Flags:
     - Chin lifted/jutting forward → Neck tension, fatigue
     - Looking down at feet → Poor posture chain reaction
     - Head bobbing → Core weakness
   • GREEN Flag: Neutral cervical spine, eyes on horizon 20-30m ahead

---

🟣 UNIVERSAL CHECKS (Apply to ALL angles):

6. SHOULDER TENSION & ELEVATION:
   • Visual Check: Are shoulders hiked up toward ears? Are they rounded forward?
   • RED Flag: Raised/tense shoulders = Energy waste, upper trap fatigue
   • GREEN Flag: Relaxed, dropped shoulders with natural oscillation
   
7. FACIAL TENSION & BREATHING:
   • Visual Check: Is the face strained? Jaw clenched? Mouth tightly closed?
   • RED Flag: Visible tension = Inefficient oxygen uptake, mental fatigue
   • Note: This is subtle but valuable for elite analysis
   
8. ASYMMETRY DETECTION (Critical for Injury Prevention):
   • Visual Check: Compare left vs. right for ANY differences in:
     - Arm swing height/amplitude
     - Stride length
     - Hip height during stance
     - Shoulder rotation
   • RED Flag: ANY visible asymmetry = Compensation pattern = Injury risk

BIOMECHANICAL CHAIN LOGIC (Interconnected Fault Detection)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Running is a kinetic chain. When one link fails, compensatory patterns emerge elsewhere.
You MUST actively search for related faults - do not analyze body parts in isolation.

🔗 CAUSAL CHAIN MAPPINGS:

IF you detect: HIP DROP (Trendelenburg Sign)
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Weak gluteus medius on stance side
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Knee valgus (inward collapse) on same leg
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Opposite arm swinging wider for balance
└─→ SEARCH FOR: Crossover gait (feet landing toward midline)

IF you detect: CROSSOVER GAIT (Narrow Stance)
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Pelvic/core instability
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Hip drop on swing leg
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Arm flailing or high guard for balance
└─→ SEARCH FOR: IT band pain risk (excessive friction)

IF you detect: HEEL STRIKE (Dorsiflexed Landing)
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Overstriding OR lack of forward lean
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Locked knee at impact (straight leg)
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Backward lean or vertical trunk
└─→ SEARCH FOR: Shin splints, knee pain indicators

IF you detect: VERTICAL/UPRIGHT TRUNK (No Forward Lean)
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Fear of falling OR lack of hip extension
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Overstriding (foot landing ahead of knee)
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Heel striking
└─→ SEARCH FOR: "Sitting in bucket" (hips behind shoulders)

IF you detect: ARM CROSSING MIDLINE
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Torso rotation OR pelvic instability
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Torso twisting excessively
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Hip drop (compensating with upper body)
└─→ SEARCH FOR: Lower back strain indicators

IF you detect: HIGH GUARD ARMS (Hands at Chest)
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Upper body tension OR fatigue
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Elevated/tense shoulders
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Shortened stride (compensation for poor mechanics)
└─→ SEARCH FOR: Neck strain, tight jaw

IF you detect: LOW HEEL RECOVERY (Shuffle Stride)
├─→ PRIMARY CAUSE: Weak hip flexors OR tight hip extensors
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Overstriding (reaching for distance)
├─→ EXPECT TO SEE: Vertical trunk (no forward momentum)
└─→ SEARCH FOR: Slow cadence, heavy foot strike
