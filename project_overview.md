# MyNextPR - Landing Page Project Overview

## 1. Project Mission & Value Proposition

**MyNextPR** is an AI-powered SaaS platform designed to **democratize professional running gait analysis**.

### The Problem
*   Professional gait analysis is expensive, inaccessible, and often intimidating.
*   Runners lack actionable feedback on their form, leading to injuries and plateaued performance.

### The Solution
*   **Accessible Coaching:** Users simply upload a photo or video still.
*   **Vintage Engineering Aesthetic:** The output isn't just a chart; it's a stylized "Davinci-style" vintage engineering blueprint that makes the analysis feel authoritative, timeless, and premium ("World Class Coach").
*   **Simple & Powerful:** A 2-step AI pipeline (Google Gemini) performs biomechanical detection followed by visual annotation.
*   **Privacy Aware:** Built with user privacy and usage limits in mind.

### Key Pitch Points for Landing Page
*   "Transform your running form with a single photo."
*   "World-class coaching insights, instantly."
*   "See your run like an engineer sees a machine."
