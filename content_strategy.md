# Content Strategy: Scroll & Discover

**Core Concept:** A minimalist "Scrollytelling" experience. The page starts quiet and intriguing, inviting the user to scroll to reveal the depth of the platform.

**Mission:** "No one should ever get injured again."
**Starting Point:** Professional Running Gait Analysis.
**Future:** Universal Biomechanics (Sitting, Walking, Living).

---

## 1. The Flow (Fold by Fold)

### Fold 1: The Hook (Minimalist Hero)
*   **Visual:** A single, cinematic, slightly abstract "Scanner" beam analyzing a runner's silhouette. Dark void background.
*   **Headline:** "See the invisible."
*   **Subhead:** "Transform a simple photo into world-class biomechanical insight."
*   **Action:** A subtle animated "Scroll to Discover" indicator (e.g., a mouse wheel icon or arrow).
*   **Vibe:** Mysterious, Premium, Scientific.

### Fold 2: The Problem (The "Why")
*   **Content:** "Pain is a warning. Usually, it comes too late."
*   **Narrative:** Most injuries are preventable. They stem from habits we can't see—how we land, how we sit, how we lean.
*   **Visual:** A "Glitch" effect on a runner's knee or back, symbolizing potential injury.

### Fold 3: The Product (Running First)
*   **Headline:** "We start with the run."
*   **Visual:** The "Before" image (Raw Photo) transitions into the "After" image (Vintage Blueprint) as the user scrolls.
*   **Key Insight:** "Your phone is now a biomechanics lab."

### Fold 4: Case Studies (The "Showcase")
*   **Format:** Horizontal scroll or "Deck of Cards" layout.
*   **Card 1: The Knee Saver**
    *   *Insight:* "Runner felt fine, but the Blueprint detected 'Valgus Collapse' (inward knee buckling)."
    *   *Outcome:* "Preempted IT Band syndrome before the first pain signal."
*   **Card 2: The Efficiency Unlock**
    *   *Insight:* "Overstriding detected. Braking forces were slowing them down."
    *   *Outcome:* "Corrected stride = 10% faster with less effort."
*   **Card 3: The Posture Fix**
    *   *Insight:* "Excessive forward lean causing lower back strain."
    *   *Outcome:* "Simple cue to 'run tall' eliminated post-run stiffness."

### Fold 5: The Mission (The Future)
*   **Headline:** "Beyond the Track."
*   **Copy:** "Running is just the beginning. Imagine analyzing your posture at your desk. Your gait while walking to coffee. Your form in the gym."
*   **Vision:** "A world where muscular pain is solved by a picture. 100% preventable."

### Fold 6: The Call to Action
*   **Headline:** "Analyze your run today."
*   **Action:** [Upload Photo] (Primary Button)
*   **Trust:** "3 Free Analyses. No Credit Card Required."

---

## 2. Tone & Voice
*   **Engineer-Poet:** Precision of an engineer, empathy of a coach.
*   **Actionable:** "Don't just show me data, tell me what to fix."
*   **Confidence:** "We don't guess. We measure."

## 3. Key Vocabulary
*   *Preempt* (vs Prevent)
*   *Blueprint* (vs Report)
*   *Structure* (vs Form)
*   *Invisible* (vs Hidden)
